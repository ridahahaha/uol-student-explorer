About the university

A community built

From engineering and computing to business, arts and health sciences, students can explore a path that reflects their interests.

Questions?

Useful answers before you begin.

Is this the official university website?

No. This is an independent student project by Rida Nawaz, built to practise HTML and CSS. Visit uol.edu.pk for official information.

How can I apply for admission?

Visit the official admissions portal, choose your programme and follow the current application instructions.

Where can I check fees and eligibility?

Check the university's current programme information or contact its admissions office. Fees and eligibility can vary by programme and intake.

Does the student form submit an application?

No. The form demonstrates browser validation and a next-steps message. It has no backend and does not submit, store or email student details.

Where is the Lahore campus?

The listed address is 1-Km Defence Road, Near Bhuptian Chowk, Lahore, Pakistan 54792. Confirm visit arrangements with the university.
